This post will be mainly about my experience and recent very entertaining video games session I had. The goal of this writing is to organize my thoughts and maybe inspire some grownups that wish to play again!

## My past with video games

My journey started when I was very young. At 7 I got the brand new Nintendo DS (the red one). Playing countless hours when parentes authorized to play, games varied from tactical ones and more casual one like pokemon and mario style games. 

It also introduced me "online" playing with my brothers and cousins when we all played on the same internet network at our grand parents house doing "Catch me if you can" in a race game. We played all night in our grand father office while the adults had dinner and enjoyed their time without the kids!

At the same time until I was around 11 I played on the family computer in the salon. I played only MMORPG games including Dofus and 4Story.

And then came Minecraft.
