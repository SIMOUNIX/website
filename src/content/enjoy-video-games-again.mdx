This post is mainly about my experience and the very entertaining video game sessions I had recently. The goal of this writing is to organize my thoughts and maybe inspire some grownups who wish to play again!

## My past with video games

My journey started when I was very young. At 7 I got the brand new Nintendo DS (the red one). I played countless hours whenever my parents allowed it. The games varied from tactical ones to more casual ones like Pokemon and Mario style games.

It also introduced me to "online" playing with my brothers and cousins. We all played on the same internet network at our grandparents' house, doing "Catch me if you can" in a race game. We played all night in our grandfather's office while the adults had dinner and enjoyed their time without the kids!

At the same time, until I was around 11, I played on the family computer in the living room. I only played MMORPG games, including Dofus and 4Story.

And then came Minecraft.

## Minecraft, the savior of Covid 19

<Image src="/images/mc_youtube.png" alt="My Minecraft videos" width={400} />

The game I spent the most time on is without a doubt Minecraft, to the extent that I made YouTube videos on it and sold thumbnails and 3D art to people online. Massive multiplayer servers are where I met a lot of my online friends, and some of them I saw IRL later!

The best part was the friends. We spent so many evenings together on the server, building, exploring and talking about everything. Some nights we just did not want to stop, and we played until very late. That was a real night of dopamine, and I miss it!

## When it stopped being fun

I played a lot of hours, for years. And then slowly I started feeling less and less into video games. I would launch a game and get bored very fast, to the point where I could not play for 15 minutes straight. Games that I loved before just felt boring.

I still had some good periods though. Sometimes lauching Minecraft or Valheim for 2 weeks and played like crazy! Griding during nights and getting tired for better equipments... It felt like the old days, but then it faded again.

## Why I came back

Recently, with social media and all that, I realized scrolling feels a lot like losing time. At the end of the day my brain was just becoming slower and slower, without thinking about anything.

This is where video games came back. They made me think. I tried to solve riddles and problems without looking for the solution online, and to understand the best builds for my type of gameplay. It is the opposite of scrolling, and I finish my sessions feeling good instead of empty. They are now my "escape" time and a way of enjoying free time without feeling the need to work to not feel like a looser in the bed at the end of the day!

## Conclusion

If you grew up with video games and lost the taste for them, give them another chance. Pick a game that makes you think, try not to look up the solutions, and play with a friend if you can. This is highly subjective to mentality and relationship with game in general but when something was abused it can be hard to enjoy it again!
